Privacy Policy

SoundStage VR (Meta Quest and Windows) · Last updated November 18, 2026

SoundStage VR ("SoundStage", "the App") is a free virtual-reality music studio. It is maintained by James Surine ("I", "me") from the open-source SoundStage project, which Google LLC and Logan Olson released under the Apache 2.0 license. The "Hard Light Labs" name shown in the App comes from the original project; that company has no part in this version or in this policy.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact me at soundstagevr@gmail.com.

1. What information does SoundStage collect?

In short: nothing, unless you use internet multiplayer. Then Unity Gaming Services, which connects the players, receives your IP address and an anonymous ID for your install of the App.

Used on your device only. SoundStage has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your scenes, samples, recordings, settings and the App's log files are stored on your headset or PC and are never sent to me. The App uses these device features only while you use them:

  • Microphone (the Microphone device): sound is processed on your device. It is saved only if you record it, and is never streamed to anyone.
  • Hand and controller tracking, head position: used to draw and control the studio.
  • Files you add (your own samples and images): read from your device's storage.

Local multiplayer (same network): headsets and PCs on your local network find each other and exchange data directly. Nothing goes over the internet or to me.

Internet multiplayer (optional; you agree to its terms in the App before using it): players are connected through Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay), run by Unity Technologies. When you use it, Unity receives:

DataWhy
An anonymous player ID created for your install of the App (not your name, email or Meta account) To let you create or join a session
Your IP address To route the session's traffic through Unity's relay servers
Session details: a scrambled form of the room code, the number of players, the App's network version and the connection codes the App needs So players with the same room code find each other
The session's traffic: the scene, samples it uses, your head and hand movements, notes and control changes Passed through the relay to the other players; Unity does not keep it

I do not receive any of this. Unity acts as a service provider (a "processor" under GDPR) for the App; see Unity's privacy policy. Voice chat is not part of the App.

2. Who else can see my information?

In short: the people in your session, and Unity as described above. I never sell or share your information.
  • Other players in your session receive the scene you're in, including copies of the samples and recordings it uses, which they can keep and save. They see your avatar's head and hands. Over the internet they don't see your IP address (traffic goes through Unity's relay). Anyone with a room code can join that room, so share codes only with people you trust and share only samples you have the right to share.
  • Unity Technologies, to run internet sessions (section 1).
  • Meta runs the Quest platform and the Horizon Store; their handling of your data is covered by Meta's own privacy policy. SoundStage does not read your Meta account information.
  • When required by law, I would disclose what I hold, which is nothing beyond emails you send me.

3. How long is information kept?

Unity keeps the personal data from internet sessions (IP address, player ID in logs) for up to 30 days. A session's room listing disappears shortly after its last player leaves. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Emails you send me are kept as long as needed to answer them.

4. How is it kept safe?

Internet sessions are encrypted (DTLS) between your device and Unity's relay, and rooms can only be found and joined with their room code. Files received from other players are checked (exact size, content fingerprint and format) before the App uses them, and are never run as programs. No system is perfectly secure, so share room codes carefully.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, Brazil's LGPD or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Because the only personal data is the anonymous ID and IP address held by Unity:

  • Use FORGET MY INTERNET ID on the App's internet multiplayer terms page (settings: MULTIPLAYER: INTERNET, then MULTIPLAYER ON, then TERMS) to delete your anonymous ID from Unity's service. A new one is made the next time you use internet multiplayer.
  • Or email me at soundstagevr@gmail.com and I'll ask Unity to delete or provide the data linked to your install (the same terms page shows your ID so you can include it).
  • Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored on your device.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

SoundStage is not directed at children under 13, and internet multiplayer is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). I don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used internet multiplayer, contact me and I'll arrange deletion.

7. International users

Unity's relay servers are in several regions; the App picks one near the players. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country, under Unity's safeguards for international transfers.

8. Do-not-track and California residents

SoundStage doesn't track you, so there's nothing to opt out of. I do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and the App asks you to agree again before internet multiplayer if its terms changed.

10. Terms for internet sessions

These are the terms you agree to in the App before using internet multiplayer:

  • Anyone with a room code can join that room. Share codes only with people you trust; there is no moderation.
  • Share only samples, recordings and other content you have the right to share. You are responsible for what you share; other players can keep copies.
  • Don't use SoundStage to share unlawful, hateful or harassing content.
  • Sessions end after 10 minutes with nobody playing, or after 3 hours; up to 4 players; samples over 5 MB are not sent over the internet.
  • Internet multiplayer is a free, best-effort service provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, and may change or stop at any time. To the extent the law allows, I am not liable for any loss or damage from using it, including content shared by other players.
